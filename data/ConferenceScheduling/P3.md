# P3 / T3 — Measured task: parallel constraint analysis

## Objective

Build a valid two-room programme from two independent constraint analyses executed in parallel. This task is measured and receives no user input at runtime.

## Situation

The conference runs from 09:00 to 12:00. A conference-wide break occupies both rooms from 10:20 to 10:40.

Rooms:

- `Auditorium`: capacity 120; projector and video-conferencing equipment.
- `SeminarRoom`: capacity 50; projector; no video-conferencing equipment.

Presentations:

| Presentation | Speaker | Duration | Audience | Equipment | Availability |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Reliable AI Systems | Alice Martin | 40 min | 90 | projector | 09:00–12:00 |
| Efficient Scientific Workflows | Bruno Lee | 40 min | 45 | projector | 09:00–12:00 |
| Reproducible Experiments | Carla Diaz | 45 min | 80 | video conferencing | 10:40–12:00 |
| Open Research Data | David Kim | 30 min | 70 | projector | must finish by 10:20 |
| Human-Centred Evaluation | Elena Rossi | 50 min | 45 | projector | 09:40–12:00 |
| Research Software Careers | Farid Noor | 35 min | 35 | none | 09:00–12:00 |

Each presentation occurs exactly once. A room and a speaker can host at most one presentation at a time. A presentation must fit within one continuous availability interval, the room capacity must cover its audience, and required equipment must be present.

## Agents

![P3 architecture](/data/ConferenceScheduling/images/P3.svg)

### `ConferenceContextAgent`

- Purpose: `present the conference situation and its factual information.`
- TaskDescription: State only the hours, fixed break, room capacities and equipment, and each presentation's speaker, duration, audience, equipment, and availability. Do not formulate a problem, analyze constraints, propose a schedule, or explain later agents.
- ExpectedOutput: A complete factual conference brief, with no analysis or schedule.

### `TimeConstraintAnalyzerAgent`

- Purpose: `identify the valid time constraints.`
- TaskDescription: Analyze only conference hours, presentation durations, the break, speaker availability, and temporal non-overlap. Do not assign rooms and do not analyze capacities or equipment.
- ExpectedOutput: A presentation-by-presentation list of valid temporal windows plus the global temporal rules.

### `RoomConstraintAnalyzerAgent`

- Purpose: `identify the valid room assignments.`
- TaskDescription: Analyze only room capacities, equipment, and room non-overlap requirements. Do not choose times and do not analyze speaker availability.
- ExpectedOutput: For each presentation, list the eligible room or rooms and the reason for any exclusion.

### `SchedulePlannerAgent`

- Purpose: `build a valid conference schedule.`
- TaskDescription: Combine both independent analyses and schedule every presentation exactly once while satisfying all temporal and room constraints.
- ExpectedOutput: A stable Markdown table with `Start`, `End`, `Room`, `Presentation`, and `Speaker` columns, sorted by start time and then room.

## Orchestration

- Mode: hybrid with a parallel block
- Order: `ConferenceContextAgent -> (TimeConstraintAnalyzerAgent || RoomConstraintAnalyzerAgent) -> SchedulePlannerAgent`
- Parallelism: both analyzer agents start from the same complete context output and execute independently. Neither analyzer consumes the other's output.
- Join: `SchedulePlannerAgent` starts only after both analyses finish and receives both outputs.
- Runtime input: none
