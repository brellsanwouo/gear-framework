# P0 — Scenario presentation

## Objective

Present the conference facts and verify the operation of the first agent. Do not solve a scheduling problem. This task is not measured and receives no user input at runtime.

## Situation

The conference takes place from 09:00 to 12:00 in `MainHall`. Three presentations are announced in this order: `Reliable AI Systems` by Alice Martin (40 minutes), `Efficient Scientific Workflows` by Bruno Lee (40 minutes), and `Reproducible Experiments` by Carla Diaz (45 minutes). A conference-wide break is fixed from 10:20 to 10:40. `MainHall` has 120 seats, a projector, and video-conferencing equipment.

## Agent

![P0 architecture](/data/ConferenceScheduling/images/P0.svg)

### `ConferenceContextAgent`

- Purpose: `present the conference situation and its factual information.`
- TaskDescription: State only the available conference facts: operating hours, room, presentations, speakers, durations, fixed break, order, capacity, and equipment. Do not formulate a problem to solve, propose a solution, assign times to presentations, or describe any other agent.
- ExpectedOutput: A concise factual conference brief containing all supplied facts and no schedule.

## Orchestration

- Mode: sequential
- Order: `ConferenceContextAgent`
- Runtime input: none

## Expected output

A factual brief that repeats the situation above without adding a proposed programme.
