# P2 / T2 — Familiarization: planning and validation

## Objective

Build and explicitly validate a conference programme. This familiarization task is not measured and receives no user input at runtime.

## Situation

The conference runs from 09:00 to 12:00 in `MainHall`. Presentations must keep this order: `Reliable AI Systems` by Alice Martin (40 minutes), `Efficient Scientific Workflows` by Bruno Lee (40 minutes), `Reproducible Experiments` by Carla Diaz (45 minutes), and `Open Research Data` by David Kim (30 minutes). The fixed break is 10:20–10:40. Carla Diaz is available from 10:40 onward; all other speakers are available throughout the conference.

## Agents

![P2 architecture](/data/ConferenceScheduling/images/P2.svg)

### `ConferenceContextAgent`

- Purpose: `present the conference situation and its factual information.`
- TaskDescription: State only the conference hours, room, ordered presentations, speakers, durations, break, and speaker availabilities. Do not formulate a problem, propose a solution, construct a programme, or explain later agents.
- ExpectedOutput: A complete factual conference brief, with no schedule.

### `SchedulePlannerAgent`

- Purpose: `build a valid conference schedule.`
- TaskDescription: Assign start and end times to all presentations from the context. Preserve order and respect conference hours, the fixed break, durations, and Carla Diaz's availability.
- ExpectedOutput: A complete schedule table with `Start`, `End`, `Room`, `Presentation`, and `Speaker` columns.

### `ScheduleValidatorAgent`

- Purpose: `check that the schedule respects all constraints.`
- TaskDescription: Check conference hours, exact durations, presentation order, room overlaps, the fixed break, and speaker availability. Return exactly `VALID` when every constraint is satisfied; otherwise return `INVALID` followed by a precise list of violations.
- ExpectedOutput: `VALID`, or `INVALID` and a list of concrete violations.

## Orchestration

- Mode: sequential
- Order: `ConferenceContextAgent -> SchedulePlannerAgent -> ScheduleValidatorAgent`
- Data flow: the planner receives the context; the validator receives the context and the proposed schedule.
- Runtime input: none
